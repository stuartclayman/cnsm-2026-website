Instructions for Session Chairs

Guidelines and procedures for chairing technical sessions at CNSM 2026.

1. Presentation Upload via QR Code

Each room will be equipped with a projector connected to a laptop containing a PowerPoint presentation with slides for each of the sessions. The session chair should display the slide corresponding to his session 15mn before the beginning of the session. Presenters should scan the QR code displayed on the slide to upload their presentations.

Please arrive in your assigned room at least 15 minutes before the session begins and open the corresponding session slide. This will allow presenters to scan the QR code and upload their presentations before the session starts and give you time to download the presentations.

The download link for the presentations is provided in the PowerPoint notes of the same slide. For security reasons, the use of USB drives is discouraged.

2. Presenter Biographies

Presenters have been asked to include a brief biography on the first slide of their presentations. Therefore, session chairs do not need to request presenters' biographies separately, as this information will be available on the first slide of each presentation.

3. Presentation Duration

The allocated time for each presentation is as follows:

  • Mini-conference papers: 15 minutes presentation + 5 minutes for Q&A
  • Full papers: 20 minutes presentation + 5 minutes for Q&A
  • Short papers: 10 minutes presentation + 5 minutes for Q&A

Please make sure to enforce the schedule to ensure that all papers receive equal presentation time.

4. Reporting No-Shows

If a scheduled presenter does not show up for their presentation, please inform CNSM 2026 TPC Co-Chairs, Mohamed Faten Zhani (mohamed.zhani@kfupm.edu.sa) and Molka Gharbaoui (molka.gharbaoui@santannapisa.it) and provide the session name, the paper's title, and scheduled presentation time.
