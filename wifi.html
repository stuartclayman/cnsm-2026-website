Wi-Fi Access Guidelines

Information and instructions to connect to the University of Alcalá Wi-Fi network during CNSM 2026.

1. Network Details

For security reasons and to ensure controlled internet access, shared-key networks are not available. Instead, the University provides a captive portal network that requires individual email registration to verify connections.

  • Network Name (SSID): visitantes-uah
  • Availability: October 26 to October 30, 2026
  • Coverage Areas: Salón de Actos and nearby corridors, Salón de Grados, Sala Juan de la Cierva, Sala Francisco Coello, Sala Torres Quevedo, and South common areas (SA5AyB).

2. Connection Instructions

Please follow these steps to connect to the visitantes-uah network:

  1. Disconnect from other networks: Ensure your device is not connected to any other Wi-Fi, mobile data, or ethernet network.
  2. Connect to the network: Select the visitantes-uah Wi-Fi network on your device and click "Connect".
  3. Access the Captive Portal: A browser window should open automatically. If it does not, manually navigate to https://clearpass.uah.es or any other website to trigger the redirect.
  4. Register: If you do not have credentials yet, click on "Regístrate" (Register).
  5. Enter Details: Provide your email address and full name. Accept the terms of use and click "Registrar" (Register).
  6. Check your Email: Your connection credentials will be sent to the email address you provided. Note: Please check all folders, including Spam or Junk, just in case.
  7. Log in: Click "Conéctate" (Connect) or return to https://clearpass.uah.es to enter your new credentials and click "Iniciar sesión" (Log in).

3. Important Rules and Limitations

  • Once connected, you will be redirected to the UAH homepage and will have internet access for 24 hours.
  • You can connect a maximum of three devices using the same access credentials.
  • Your credentials are strictly personal and non-transferable. Do not share them with anyone, as the requester is responsible for any inappropriate use linked to their email address.
